AgentHelm

"MCP-native governance & audit-trail platform for multi-agent AI systems."

Problem

Autonomous agents invoke tools and call other agents with no durable record of who did what, when, and why — making incidents impossible to audit after the fact.

What was built

An MCP-native control plane: safety boundaries, human-in-the-loop approvals, live traces, and a hash-chained provenance ledger. Published as Python + Node.js SDKs on PyPI and npm.

Concrete result

✓ Shipped on PyPI + npm · live demo at agenthelm.online · governance primitives adopted across my freelance agent engagements.
